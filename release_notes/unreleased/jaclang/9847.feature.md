- **Feature: emailers send Reply-To and attachments**: `Emailer.send_email` takes optional `reply_to` and `attachments` (`EmailAttachment`: filename, bytes, MIME type, optional `content_id`). The SMTP emailer sets `Reply-To`, shows files with a `content_id` inside the HTML body as `cid:<content_id>` (inline images), and attaches the rest. Sends without them are unchanged.
